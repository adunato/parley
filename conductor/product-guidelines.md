# Product Guidelines: Parley

## 1. Narrative Style
*   **Configurable Prose:** The prose style (Descriptive, Concise, etc.) is a user-configurable setting, allowing players to tailor the reading experience to their preference.
*   **Consequence over Constraint:** When players make "out of character" choices, the engine should prioritize **Reactive Consequences**. The world and NPCs should react realistically to the inconsistency, and character stats should reflect the shift, rather than the engine forcing consistency.

## 2. Visual Identity & UI
*   **Minimalist Core:** The primary UI philosophy is **Minimalist and Clean**, ensuring the story remains the focus through clear typography and effective use of whitespace.
*   **Thematic Exceptions:** Specific UI components, such as a character's smartphone or in-game documents, may adopt a more **Thematic and Immersive** style to enhance the simulation.
*   **Data Visibility:** Despite the minimalist aesthetic, the UI must efficiently present a fair amount of character and world statistics to support the depth of the simulation.

## 3. Content & Safety
*   **User-Defined Limits:** The application empowers players to set their own safety and content boundaries at the start of the game, ensuring the experience aligns with their personal comfort levels.

## 4. NPC Interaction
*   **Distinctive Voices:** NPC dialogue must prioritize **Distinctive Voices**. Each character's speech patterns, vocabulary, and tone should uniquely reflect their procedurally generated traits and background.
