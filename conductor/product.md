# Product Guide: Parley

## 1. Vision
Parley is a web-based text adventure game that leverages AI to create a dynamic and immersive storytelling experience. It targets role-playing enthusiasts who value deep character customization and complex, evolving narratives. The game blends the intricacies of a modern life simulation with the freedom of open-world exploration.

## 2. Target Audience
*   **Role-Playing Enthusiasts:** Players who enjoy crafting detailed personas, managing stats, and seeing their choices impact the world and their character's development.

## 3. Key Features
*   **AI-Driven Storytelling:** The narrative engine dynamically generates story segments based on user input, ensuring a unique experience for every playthrough.
*   **Procedural Character Generation:** Characters are created with detailed traits, backgrounds, and personalities, powered by procedural generation algorithms.
*   **Relationship Management:** A robust system tracks interactions with NPCs, influencing how they react and evolve alongside the player.

## 4. Gameplay Goals
*   **Life Simulation:** Players aim to develop a successful life for their character, managing career paths, social circles, and personal growth.
*   **Exploration:** The game encourages players to explore the game world, uncovering secrets, unlocking new locations, and discovering hidden lore.

## 5. Setting & Tone
*   **Genre:** Modern Life Simulation.
*   **Tone:** The narrative balances a Serious and Realistic foundation with moments of Lightheartedness and Humor, creating a grounded yet entertaining experience.
